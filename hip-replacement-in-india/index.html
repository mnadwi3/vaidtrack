Hip Replacement Options

personal_injuryFor Widespread Hip Joint Damage

Total Hip Replacement (THR)

A long-term solution for severe hip pain.

Total Hip Replacement in India replaces both the damaged femoral head (the ball) and the worn acetabulum (the socket) with an artificial ball-and-socket joint, restoring pain-free movement for patients with severe hip arthritis or joint damage.

May be suitable for:

  • checkSevere hip arthritis or joint damage
  • checkDamage to both the femoral head and socket
  • checkPersistent hip pain and stiffness
  • checkLimited improvement with non-surgical treatments

personal_injuryFor Certain Hip Fractures

Partial Hip Replacement (Hemiarthroplasty)

A targeted recovery after a hip fracture.

Partial Hip Replacement (Hemiarthroplasty) in India replaces only the damaged femoral head while preserving the natural acetabulum, commonly recommended for certain hip fractures in older patients, for a faster, more targeted recovery.

May be suitable for:

  • checkCertain hip fractures in older patients
  • checkHealthy, undamaged acetabulum (socket)
  • checkWanting a faster, more targeted recovery
  • checkLower activity level and demand on the joint

personal_injuryFor Younger, Active Patients

Hip Resurfacing Surgery

Preserving more of your natural bone.

Hip Resurfacing Surgery in India reshapes and caps the femoral head with a smooth metal covering instead of removing it, preserving more of the natural femoral bone, an option often suited to younger, active patients with strong bone quality.

May be suitable for:

  • checkYounger, more active patients
  • checkStrong, healthy bone quality
  • checkWanting to preserve natural femoral bone
  • checkDesire to return to higher-impact activity

Revision Hip Replacement

Restoring stability after a failed implant.

Revision Hip Replacement in India replaces a previously implanted hip prosthesis that has loosened, worn out, dislocated, or become infected, restoring stability and pain-free movement through a more complex, tailored procedure.

May be suitable for:

  • checkLoosened, worn, or dislocated implant
  • checkInfection in a previous hip replacement
  • checkPersistent pain after prior surgery
  • checkComplex cases needing tailored bone reconstruction
